TransactIQ — Forecasting India’s Journey Beyond Cash with UPI

Modeled and forecast UPI transaction growth in India using historical data. The analysis explores long-term adoption, seasonal fluctuations, and shifts around the COVID-19 period, using an ARIMA-based model to forecast transaction volumes and examine the rise of digital payments.
